How do I get my CSV?
  1. Use chase.com on a computer. The Chase mobile app only gives PDF statements.
  2. Sign in and open your credit card account.
  3. Click the download icon above your transactions ("Download account activity").
  4. For file type, choose "Spreadsheet (Excel, CSV)", pick a date range, and click Download.
  5. Then drag the downloaded file here, or click "Choose a file".

Got a PDF? That's a monthly statement. Spend Snapshot needs the activity download (CSV) instead.
